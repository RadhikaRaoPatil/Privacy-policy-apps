Calculate Anything: Finance Calculator

Calculate Anything: Finance Calculator ("Calculate Anything", "we", "our", or "us") is an Android application that provides financial and utility calculators.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you enter into calculators

You may enter values such as loan amounts, interest rates, investment amounts, dates, income, expenses, and other calculator inputs. These values are used to perform calculations and display results.

Calculator history and favorites are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system or cloud database for this information in V1.

1.2 Account information

Calculate Anything does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, provide a phone number, or sign in to use the app.

1.3 Advertising and device information

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users. AdMob and its related SDKs may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising identifiers, app/device information, diagnostics, and ad interaction information, subject to Google's policies and the choices available to users.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising and to support advertising personalization where permitted.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about partner sites and apps.

Premium users who have successfully purchased the V1 lifetime Premium product are not shown advertisements by Calculate Anything.

1.4 Analytics

The app may use analytics and crash-reporting services when these services are enabled in a released version. If enabled, those services may collect technical and usage information such as app events, device information, diagnostics, and crash information.

The exact services enabled for a particular release will be reflected in the Google Play Data Safety disclosure and this policy.

1.5 Currency Converter

The Currency Converter uses a third-party exchange-rate service to obtain exchange-rate data. The app does not send calculator history, favorites, account credentials, or payment information to the exchange-rate service as part of the currency conversion request.

The third-party service may receive technical network information, such as the user's IP address, as part of normal internet communication. The app caches the most recently successful exchange-rate data locally so the converter can continue to provide useful results when the network is unavailable.

1.6 Purchases

Premium is sold as a one-time digital purchase through Google Play Billing. Calculate Anything does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account information. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as necessary to:

  • provide calculator functionality;
  • save calculator history, favorites, and preferences locally;
  • provide and improve app functionality;
  • display and measure advertising for free users;
  • process and restore Premium purchases through Google Play;
  • provide analytics or crash reporting where enabled;
  • obtain currency exchange-rate data;
  • respond to support requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your calculator history or favorites.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers required for app functionality, including Google AdMob for advertising, Google Play Billing for purchases, analytics/crash-reporting providers if enabled, the currency-rate provider used by the Currency Converter, and services used when you explicitly choose to share a result or exported file.

4. Sharing Results

If you use the app's Share functionality, the app creates content and passes it to the Android sharing mechanism or an application you select. Once you choose to share content with another application or person, that recipient or service controls how the shared information is handled.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Calculator history, favorites, preferences, and cached currency-rate information are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated for a particular service.

We do not maintain a cloud account containing your calculator history or favorites in V1. Removing the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored information. Android device backups or other operating-system services may have separate data-handling practices outside our control.

6. Children's Privacy

Calculate Anything is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through an account system.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the app's design to protect information handled by our systems and third-party services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Choices

  • Use the app without creating an account.
  • Control or reset advertising preferences through available Google/Android controls.
  • Purchase Premium to remove Calculate Anything advertisements.
  • Clear locally stored app data through Android settings.
  • Choose whether to use features such as sharing or the Currency Converter.

9. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services including Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, analytics/crash-reporting services when enabled, and the currency-rate provider. Their practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will include a new effective date.
