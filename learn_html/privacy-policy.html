Privacy Policy

Learn HTML

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

Codemic ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Learn HTML mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Learn HTML.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Stored Locally

Learn HTML stores certain learning and application preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Lesson completion and learning progress
  • Quiz and practice progress
  • XP and streak information
  • Study-related progress
  • Playground data
  • App appearance preferences
  • Notification and sound preferences
  • Other application settings

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide the App's functionality.

Learn HTML does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or password to use the core learning features.

1.2 Analytics Information

Learn HTML uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality.

Analytics information may include:

  • App usage and interaction events
  • Screens viewed
  • Session information
  • App and device information
  • General usage information
  • Advertising or app-related identifiers where applicable
  • Diagnostic information

1.3 Crash and Diagnostic Information

Learn HTML uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, errors, and technical problems.

Crash reports may include:

  • Crash logs
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • App version
  • Diagnostic information
  • Information about the state of the App when a crash occurs

This information is used to diagnose technical problems, improve stability, and maintain the App.

1.4 Advertising Information

Learn HTML uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob and associated services may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • App interaction and advertising information
  • Information used to deliver, measure, and improve advertising

Learn HTML displays banner advertisements in designated areas of the App.

If you use the App's premium or ad-free functionality, advertisements may be disabled according to the functionality provided by the App.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected or generated through the App and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain Learn HTML
  • Save and display learning progress
  • Improve lessons and learning features
  • Understand how the App is used
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Improve App performance and stability
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Develop and improve future versions of the App

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

3. Third-Party Services

Learn HTML uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services and related Android services

These third-party providers process information according to their respective policies.

4. Advertising and Consent

Because Learn HTML uses Google AdMob, advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners.

Where legally required, appropriate consent mechanisms may be presented to users before personalized advertising or other regulated data processing takes place.

Users may also be able to manage advertising preferences through Google's available controls and their device settings.

5. Data Sharing

We may share information with third-party service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, analyze, secure, or monetize the App.

For example:

  • Firebase may process analytics and crash information.
  • AdMob may process advertising-related information.
  • Google Play Services may process information required for Android and Google Play functionality.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Retention

Information stored locally by Learn HTML remains on your device until:

  • You delete or reset the relevant information within the App, where available;
  • You clear the App's data; or
  • You uninstall the App.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and AdMob is retained according to the respective service policies and applicable retention settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App and use established third-party services and security mechanisms where appropriate.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Learn HTML is intended as a general educational application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that requires removal, please contact us using the information below.

9. Your Choices

Depending on your device and the features available in the App, you may be able to:

  • Reset your learning progress
  • Clear playground data
  • Change notification preferences
  • Change sound preferences
  • Change light, dark, or system appearance
  • Manage applicable advertising preferences through Google controls
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored application data

10. External Services

The App may use or link to services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those services separately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
