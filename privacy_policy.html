Privacy Policy

Thank you for using DailyVerse Hub. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

DailyVerse Hub does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Local Data Storage

To provide a better user experience, the app stores certain information locally on your device, including:

This information remains only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Permissions

DailyVerse Hub may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications – Used only to deliver daily quote reminders if enabled by you.

These permissions are optional and are only used for the intended app functionality.

Third-Party Services

DailyVerse Hub may use trusted Flutter packages to provide app functionality, such as:

  • Sharing quotes

  • Displaying local notifications

The app does not use these packages to collect or transmit your personal information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.

Children's Privacy

DailyVerse Hub is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
